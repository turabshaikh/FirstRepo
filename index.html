Meet your trainer

Learn from an engineer who works with the platform.

I'm Turab Shaikh, an infrastructure professional focused on Nutanix administration, virtualization, and enterprise platform operations. My experience spans cluster implementation, day-to-day administration, data protection, migration, monitoring, and production troubleshooting.

My goal as a trainer is to make complex infrastructure concepts easier to understand—starting with the physical layer, connecting each platform component, and then applying that knowledge to operational tasks and architecture decisions.

Training is designed to explain not only which step to perform, but also why it matters, what can fail, how to investigate, and how to validate the result.

Curriculum overview

Skills you can take into the data center.

A practical curriculum built around the technologies and responsibilities commonly found in Nutanix environments.

01 / FOUNDATION

Nutanix HCI architecture

Understand nodes, blocks, disks, CVMs, the hypervisor, distributed storage, resiliency, and the end-to-end architecture.

02 / PLATFORM

AOS and AHV administration

Explore core services, storage containers, virtual networks, VM operations, host maintenance, and cluster lifecycle tasks.

03 / MANAGEMENT

Prism Element and Central

Navigate management interfaces, alerts, capacity, performance, inventory, health checks, and operational workflows.

04 / RESILIENCY

Data protection and DR

Work through snapshots, protection policies, replication, recovery, NearSync, Metro Availability, and DR testing concepts.

05 / MIGRATION

VMware to Nutanix migration

Plan discovery, compatibility checks, network mapping, migration waves, testing, cutover, rollback, and validation.

06 / SERVICES

Nutanix Files and Self-Service

Understand file-service concepts, shares, access, capacity considerations, and self-service application and VM workflows.

07 / OPERATIONS

Monitoring and performance

Interpret cluster health, capacity, latency, IOPS, throughput, alerts, and performance symptoms before taking action.

08 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Production troubleshooting

Build a disciplined approach to node, disk, CVM, service, networking, VM, and cluster-health investigations.

09 / DESIGN

Architecture and sizing basics

Discuss workload requirements, cluster design considerations, fault domains, capacity planning, and recovery objectives.
